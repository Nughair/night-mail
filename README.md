Night Mail — pilot a paper plane over a moonlit city and slip letters through windows that glow gold. An eight-route campaign of rising quotas, wind and rooftop density — Harbour Row, Old Town, the fog-bound Canal District, the Mill District, the Festival Quarter under its fireworks, the Heights, the Storm Quarter — then the Dawn Run, a storm flight against the sunrise. Weather fronts drift across the later districts: fog banks that hide the windows, rain bands that shove the plane sideways. Some letters are special — express post pays double against a nine-second clock, and a fragile letter is voided by a single rooftop clip. After every route, draft one of three upgrades (a bigger mailbag, a tailwind charm, reinforced wings that survive one rooftop clip, and more) and carry it for the rest of the campaign. Every route is scored for a medal — bronze, silver, gold — kept on the campaign map; win the campaign and the Night Owl opens, an endless night that loops the districts with rising quotas. Chain deliveries into combos, skim rooftops for near-miss bonuses, and beat the Dawn Run to win.
