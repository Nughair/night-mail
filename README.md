Night Mail — pilot a paper plane over a moonlit city and slip letters through windows that glow gold. A six-route campaign: five districts of rising quotas, wind and rooftop density — Harbour Row, Old Town, the Mill District, the Heights, the Storm Quarter — then the Dawn Run, a storm flight against the sunrise. After every route, draft one of three upgrades (a bigger mailbag, a tailwind charm, reinforced wings that survive one rooftop clip, and more) and carry it for the rest of the campaign. Chain deliveries into combos, skim rooftops for near-miss bonuses, and beat the Dawn Run to win.
